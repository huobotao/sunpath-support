MilkTeaSpace Support

English version prepared: September 20, 2026. Based on the Chinese support page dated September 1, 2026.

MilkTeaSpace is an offline milk tea recipe browser. Compare 24 built-in drink examples across ten taste dimensions: tea, milk, rich cheese, fruit, chewiness, starch, grains, sweetness, acidity and freshness.

Use Search, Category and the quick filters to explore the menu. Adjust Taste preferences to rank the drinks by similarity to your preferences. Open a drink to see its Taste profile, Recipe breakdown and Ingredient guide. The app includes English and Simplified Chinese, and search works with both languages.

Contact support

Email huobotao@hotmail.com and include your device model, iOS/iPadOS/macOS version, app version, screenshots and steps to reproduce the issue.

Frequently asked questions

  • Can I use the app without internet access? Yes. The current version includes its recipe and taste data in the app. Search, filters, preference sliders and charts run on your device.
  • Why don't my selections sync between devices? The current version does not create accounts or provide cloud sync. Each device runs independently.
  • Are these official store recipes? No. The content is for exploring and comparing flavor structures. It does not represent any brand's official recipes, nutrition information or allergen information.
  • Can I use this for allergy or medical decisions? No. Before buying or making a drink, check ingredients, nutrition and allergens directly with the seller or ingredient supplier.

Privacy

The current version does not request access to location, the camera, photos or contacts, or send usage data. See the MilkTeaSpace Privacy Policy.
