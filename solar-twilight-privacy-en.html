Solar Twilight Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Help & Support · 简体中文

Solar Twilight calculates sunrise, sunset and twilight times on your device. No account is required. The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not operate a server that collects data from the app.

Location

With your permission, the app requests your location through the system's location service using When In Use authorization. Coordinates are held in memory for local calculations. The app does not write them to persistent storage or transmit them to the developer or third-party network services.

If a current location is unavailable, the app clearly labels Sydney as the default city. On iPhone, you can also choose one of seven built-in cities without granting location access.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app requests its location through the system and runs the same calculations on the watch. It uses the labeled Sydney default when location is unavailable. It does not send or synchronize location through developer-operated servers, and it does not create a cross-device user profile.

Data collection and tracking

The app does not transmit contact details, identifiers, usage data, diagnostic data or location to developer-operated services. We do not sell data or track you across apps or websites. Apple may process App Store downloads, system diagnostics, crash reports and location services under its own policies; these system services are not controlled by this app.

Opening a support or privacy link uses your browser or the system's web interface. If you email support, the information you choose to send is handled through your email service and our support mailbox. Avoid including your full address or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Permissions, retention and deletion

You can change or withdraw location permission in your device's Location Services settings. The app does not save location history or maintain a developer account database. To delete the app itself, use your device's app-management controls.
