MilkTeaSpace Privacy Policy

English version prepared: September 20, 2026. Based on the Chinese policy dated September 1, 2026.

MilkTeaSpace is an offline tool for browsing milk tea recipes and flavors. The app does not create user accounts, display advertising or use analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not use a developer-operated server to collect user data.

Data and permissions

The current version includes its drink data in the app. Search, filters, taste preference sliders, similarity comparisons and charts are processed on your device. The app does not request access to location, the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, health data or Bluetooth, and does not send the content of your interactions to the developer or third parties.

The app's privacy and support links open external web pages only when you select them. Opening a web page or choosing to send an email uses the relevant browser or email service, separately from the app's offline processing.

Data collection and tracking

The app does not collect contact information, identifiers, purchase history, usage data or diagnostic data. It does not sell data or track you across apps or websites. Apple may process App Store downloads, system diagnostics or crash reports under its own policies; these system services are not controlled by this app.

Storage and deletion

Search, filter choices and taste preferences are handled locally in memory while the app is running. The current version does not provide persistent history, cloud profiles or cross-device sync, and there is no developer-operated user account database. Removal or retention of app-related files is managed by the operating system and can differ by platform; removing the app does not necessarily remove every related file.

Contact us

For privacy questions, email huobotao@hotmail.com or visit MilkTeaSpace Support.
