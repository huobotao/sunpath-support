Solar Twilight Help & Support

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Privacy Policy · 简体中文

Solar Twilight calculates sunrise, sunset, and civil, nautical and astronomical twilight on your device. It includes an iPhone app and a companion app for a paired Apple Watch. No account or internet connection is needed for the calculations. Opening web pages or sending support email requires the relevant external service.

On iPhone

  • Use the location menu to choose Current location, Sydney, Beijing, Shanghai, San Francisco, New York, London or Tokyo.
  • Select Today or Tomorrow to view that day's sunrise, sunset and twilight times.
  • The Next card always shows the next calculated event after the current time, with its time and countdown. Selecting Tomorrow changes the daily list, not this card.
  • Built-in cities use their own time zones. Current location uses your device's current time zone.

On Apple Watch

The watch shows today's sunrise, sunset and twilight times, plus the next calculated event and countdown. It uses the watch's current location when available, or Sydney as a clearly labeled default. Use Get location to try again when the default city is shown. City selection and the Today/Tomorrow control are available on iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

  • Why does it show Sydney (default city)? A current location is not available yet, permission is off, or a location request failed. Allow location access in your device's Location Services settings, then use Refresh location on iPhone or Get location on Apple Watch. On iPhone, make sure Current location is selected; you can also use any built-in city without location access.
  • Why is the app missing from my Apple Watch? Confirm that your watch is paired with your iPhone and check whether Solar Twilight is installed in the iPhone's Watch app.
  • Why is a time missing? At high latitudes, some dates have polar day or polar night. For twilight, the sun may stay above or below a threshold all day. The app displays these states when calculated; None means that no event time was found for that entry.
  • How far ahead does Next look? It checks ten local calendar days, including today, for an event after the current time. If none is found, iPhone shows No calculated events in the next 10 days and Watch shows No upcoming event. This does not mean there will never be another event.
  • Why does the countdown say Any moment? The countdown displays hours and minutes. Any moment is shown when less than one minute remains.
  • Why do times differ from another source? Calculations use standard solar height thresholds: −18° for astronomical twilight, −12° for nautical twilight, −6° for civil twilight and −0.833° for sunrise and sunset. Terrain, elevation, atmospheric conditions and different conventions can affect observed times. Treat these calculated times as a planning reference.
  • Why does the local time look wrong? Confirm the selected city. For Current location, also check your device's date and time zone, because the app uses the device's current time zone for those coordinates.

Privacy and data

Location is used for calculations on your device and is not sent by the app to the developer or third-party network services. Read the Solar Twilight Privacy Policy.
