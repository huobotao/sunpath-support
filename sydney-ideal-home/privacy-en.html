Privacy Policy

Text updated: 20 September 2026

Privacy PolicyHelp & SupportChinese version

This policy explains how Sydney Home Concept Lab handles project content and the data involved in maps, documents, sharing and support contact. It covers the features in this version of the app. Apple services, file services, sharing destinations and mail services you choose to use may also process data under their own rules.

Projects and local calculations

Preference weights, candidate rankings and parametric home concept models are processed on your device. This version has no developer-account registration or sign-in, advertising or behavioural analytics, and no feature that uploads projects to a developer server.

The app includes 16 concept candidate sites in Sydney. Candidate metrics, scoring and the three scenarios let you compare preferences and preset assumptions. They do not assess actual properties, building obstructions, planning projects or safety conditions. The 3D homes show design intentions, not sunlight, ventilation, wind shelter or glare performance verified through measurements or simulation.

Apple Maps and network access

Maps are provided by Apple MapKit. Opening and browsing a map may request map content from Apple over the network. Apple provides the service and handles its associated data processing. Not every app feature is fully offline, and this policy does not mean that Apple processes no service requests or related data.

The map is for display only and does not contribute to concept scoring. The app does not request the device's real-time location. The candidate coordinates on the map come from built-in concept data, not your current location.

Project documents, save locations and deletion

Projects are saved as .sihome documents. A document contains the project name, creation and update times, preference weights, scenario, home prototype, selected candidate site, and dataset identity, version-verification information and binding history.

Documents are managed through the system document interface and saved in the location you choose. If you choose iCloud Drive or another file service, that provider may sync the file according to your settings. The app has no custom cloud project account or project-upload server; this does not prevent system file services from syncing documents.

Manage or delete documents at their original save location, and check Recently Deleted items, backups and other copies according to the file service you use. Uninstalling the app does not guarantee deletion of documents stored in the cloud, in other locations or with sharing recipients. The developer cannot remotely delete projects you have saved in those locations through the app.

Sharing and external links you choose to use

In layouts that provide “Share Summary”, you choose to use the system share sheet and select a destination before handing the summary to the corresponding app or service. The summary may include the project name, candidate site, scenario, home prototype, concept score and dataset information. Before sending, check that you want to disclose that information to the recipient. The summary is not automatically sent to the developer.

Data-source links, where provided, open their websites only when you click them. The concept data built into this version provides no source-website links. External websites and sharing services process data under their own rules.

Device access

This version does not implement collection through the camera, microphone or photo library, or collection of the device's real-time location. The 3D home view uses a virtual camera to display a concept model; it is not camera-based AR measurement.

Support email and privacy questions

Support email: huobotao@hotmail.com. Clicking the email link only requests that your mail app open; it does not send email automatically.

If you choose to send an email, your sender address, message and any attachments you add are provided to the mail service and support recipient to handle your request. Do not send passwords, verification codes or private documents unrelated to the issue. Cover private information in screenshots first.

To ask about or request deletion of support emails you previously chose to send, contact the same address and explain your request. Handling of messages and their backups depends on the relevant mail service's mechanisms. This is separate from deleting project documents you have saved yourself.

Website hosting

This privacy and support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when they visit a GitHub Pages site, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. This page adds no advertising, analytics scripts, forms or externally embedded resources.

For information about the hosting service's data handling, see GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Calculating project results in the app and visiting this website are separate data-processing activities.

Updates to this explanation

This explanation will be updated when the app's data handling changes. The date above identifies the text version you are reading. If you have questions, contact the support email address.

Concept results are not property, planning, engineering or safety advice. Actual decisions require official information and separate verification.
