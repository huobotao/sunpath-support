This is a concept tool for exploring home preferences. Compare preference weights and hypothetical scenarios across 16 concept candidate sites in Sydney, and view parametric 3D home prototypes.

The candidate sites are not recommendations for properties for sale, and the scores are not measurements taken on site. The app does not incorporate actual planning projects, building-obstruction analysis or safety assessments. It cannot replace official information or professional judgement.

Start with a project

  1. Create or open a .sihome project through the system document interface.
  2. In the preferences panel, adjust eight weights: water view, greenery, sunlight, quiet, privacy, access, urban energy and view stability. Choose a scenario and compare the concept rankings.
  3. Select a candidate site and review its Data Sources & Quality notes. Switch between home prototypes and rotate the 3D concept model.
  4. Use the system document interface to manage where your project is saved. To learn about data handling, open “Privacy & Support” in the preferences panel.

Understanding rankings, scenarios and 3D models

Rankings are calculated locally from built-in concept metrics and your preference weights. The three scenarios adjust metrics using preset assumptions; they do not predict actual planning proposals. Data-quality indicators are neither a guarantee that a property suits you nor a measure of prediction accuracy.

Descriptions of sunlight, ventilation, wind shelter and shading express design intentions. The 3D models have not been validated against actual sunlight, wind fields, glare or building obstructions. They are not camera-based AR measurements of a home.

If the map does not load or is incomplete

The map uses Apple MapKit. Browsing it may require a network connection and Apple Maps services. Check that your network is available, then return to the map view. Not all map content is guaranteed to be available offline. The map is for display only and does not contribute to concept scoring.

The map shows the top eight ranked candidate sites plus the currently selected site, so it does not always show all 16 sites at once. Candidate coordinates come from the built-in data; the app does not request your real-time location.

Saving, opening and deleting documents

Check your project's save location in the system document interface. If you choose iCloud Drive or another file service, whether and when a file syncs depends on that service and your settings. The app has no developer account or custom project-upload service.

If a document will not open, keep the original file. Note the error message, app version, operating-system version, and whether the file is stored locally or with a file service. Do not delete your only copy while troubleshooting.

Delete a project from the location where you saved it. You may also need to manage Recently Deleted items, backups and other copies under the file service's rules. Uninstalling the app does not guarantee deletion of cloud files or content you have already shared.

Sharing and privacy

In layouts that show “Share Summary”, tap it and choose a recipient or destination in the system share sheet. A layout without that control does not mean a project has been shared automatically. Check the project name and other information in the summary before sending it.

This version has no advertising, behavioural analytics or feature that uploads projects to a developer server. Map services, file syncing you choose and sharing you initiate may still use the network. Read the Privacy Policy for more information.

Do not send passwords, verification codes or private information unrelated to the problem. Your message and any attachments you choose to add are provided to the mail service and support recipient. See Support email and privacy questions.
